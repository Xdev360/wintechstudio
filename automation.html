What we actually do

We build the AI layer around how you already sell.

Most businesses do not have an AI problem. They have a volume problem that looks like an AI problem. Enquiries arrive faster than anyone can answer them, the same eight questions get typed out forty times a day, and the person doing the typing is also the person supposed to be running the company.

The layer we build sits exactly there. It answers what is answerable, escalates what is not, and keeps a record of both. It knows your catalogue, your prices, your delivery windows, and the way you speak to customers, because it is built on those rather than on a template with your logo dropped in.

The test we hold it to after launch is one question: how many conversations still reach a human, and are those the ones that genuinely needed a human? If the answer is wrong, we tune it until it is right.

  • Customer support AIBuilt on your actual product range, delivery terms, and refund policy. It handles the repeat questions end to end and passes anything unusual to a person with the full thread already attached.
  • Enquiry qualificationEvery message and form gets asked the two or three questions that decide whether it is worth your time, then gets routed accordingly. Serious buyers reach you warm and already understood.
  • An admin agent you can messageAsk what is pending, what sold today, or what needs attention, and get the answer in Telegram. No dashboard login, no opening a laptop at eleven at night.
  • The workflows behind itOrder confirmations, follow-ups, stock notices, receipts. The repetitive middle of the day, written once and then running on its own.

How it gets built

Narrow first, then wider as accuracy holds.

Launching an agent across the whole business on day one is how they end up switched off in month two. We go the other way.

Week one

We read the last month of your real conversations. The tone, the top questions, and the edge cases all come out of that, rather than out of our assumptions about your industry.

Week two to three

The first agent goes live on a deliberately narrow scope, usually support, where a mistake is cheap and immediately visible. You watch every conversation during this stretch.

Week four onward

Scope widens once accuracy holds: qualification next, then the admin agent, then the workflows sitting behind both. Each expansion is a decision you make with data in front of you.

Handover

You get the prompts, the routing rules, and the escalation logic in a form your team can read and edit. If we disappeared, the system would keep running and someone on your side could change it.

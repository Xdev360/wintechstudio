About

A small studio, deliberately.

Wintech Studio is a design-and-product studio in Lagos, working with businesses anywhere. We study how a company actually wins, design the technology around that, then build it: AI, product, site, brand.

01

Study first

We look at operations, demand, and where the money leaks before we write a brief. Plenty of projects change shape during that week, and a few turn out to need something smaller than what was asked for.

02

Build for impact

An AI with a tone your customers recognise. A site search engines can actually describe. A first version that can take real money. Every piece has to earn its place in the build order.

03

Stay selective

Four projects at a time. Weekly shipping only works when the person building has room to think, so we keep the number where the standard holds and turn work away when it is full.

Who you work with

You always talk to the person who owns the outcome.

There is no account layer here. The person who runs your diagnostic week is the person who writes the build map, ships the work, and picks up the phone when something breaks at eleven at night.

OI

Oladele Ibraheem

Founder · product, brand, and frontend

UI and UX, Next.js and React, and brand strategy under one hand. Runs the diagnostic, writes the map, ships the work, and owns whether it worked.

Build partners

Brought in per project, named before we start

Heavier product and AI work runs alongside a small bench of engineers and designers we have already shipped with. You know who is on your project before the first invoice, and they work to the same weekly rhythm.

How partners are chosen

Only people we have shipped a real project with before. No marketplace hires, no first-timers learning on your budget. If the right person is not free, we say the timeline moves rather than swapping in someone untested.

How weekly shipping is protected

Four projects at a time, hard cap. Scope is agreed on one page in week one, and anything added after that moves something else out rather than quietly absorbing the schedule.

What happens if something breaks

You message the founder, not a ticket queue. Post-launch, the system is handed over documented enough that your own team can change it without us.

What we do not do

We do not take work we cannot staff properly, and we do not run more than four builds at once to make a quarter look better. That is the whole reason the standard holds.

Who it’s for

Operators who want the system to catch up with the business.

Being straight about fit saves everyone a month. Here is roughly how it splits.

We build for

  • Founders who have outgrown fragmented tools and manual work
  • Businesses losing money to operational drag they can name
  • Teams that need a real product, or a first version in market fast
  • Brands whose current site undersells how good the operation is

We are a poor fit if

  • Price is the deciding factor rather than the outcome
  • You want a supplier to hand off to and never speak with
  • The real constraint is ad spend, in which case we will say so
  • Nobody on your side can make a decision inside a week
